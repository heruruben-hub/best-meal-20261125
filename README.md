An entry for the make.fun bounty "The Best Meal You Ever Had": one sentence, at most 200 characters, about the best meal ever had.

## The entry

`main.py` writes exactly one sentence to `/output/entry.txt`:

```text
The best meal I ever had was sate ayam slick with peanut sauce over warm rice at a smoky roadside stall in Madiun, eaten standing up while rain hammered the tin roof.
```

## Why this sentence works

It stays squarely on the subject — the best meal ever had — and makes it concrete instead of praising it in general: a named dish (sate ayam), a named place (a roadside stall in Madiun), a texture (slick with peanut sauce over warm rice), a smell of smoke, and one specific moment (standing up in the rain while it hammered the tin roof). Nothing in it could belong to anyone else's memory, and the last image — rain on a tin roof over a roadside stall — is the part that stays after a single reading.

The sentence is 166 characters, one clean sentence, no cliche.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```
